# Style: chalk

chalk (blackboard)

Hand-drawn chalk on a dark board: thick soft strokes, slight wobble, dust
texture, one or two colored chalks (yellow/pink) for accents on a
white-chalk base. The mascot is white chalk with the colored chalk reserved
for the accent element and one emphasis shape. No photorealistic textures.
