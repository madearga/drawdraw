# Style: snes

16-bit console key art: richer pixel art than the `pixel` style — larger
palettes (16–32 colors), simple shading within sprites, dramatic hero
poses, painterly pixel backgrounds.

- Rendering: still hard-edged pixels (no anti-aliasing), but with internal
  shading — 2–3 tones per material, subtle dithering transitions allowed.
  Backgrounds are environmental (sky bands, terrain layers, simple props)
  rather than flat voids.
- The mascot is rendered as a hero sprite: expressive pose, slight
  perspective, rim-light tone on one edge.
- Composition: widescreen key-art staging — mascot off-center, environment
  sweeping behind it. 16:9 suits this style; 1:1 works with a tighter
  vignette.
