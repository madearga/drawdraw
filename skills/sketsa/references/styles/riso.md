# Style: riso

Flat spot-color print look, like a risograph poster.

- Paper: warm cream, subtle grain. No gradients, no soft shadows — shading
  is a second flat tone or a halftone dot patch.
- Ink: every edge has an even structure-ink outline (charcoal, not pure
  black). Same weight everywhere.
- Spot colors, 2–3 per image besides paper and ink. The character carries
  one saturated accent; the scene uses the quieter colors.
- Composition: one clear metaphor, generous negative space, mascot as the
  focal point doing the action.

| Palette   | Paper    | Structure ink | Accents                     |
|-----------|----------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| default   | #F5EFE0  | #2B2B33       | #FF6C2F, #0078BF            |
| night     | #1E2430  | #0B0D12       | #FFD23F, #7FD1B9 (mascot stays the light spot) |
| blueprint | #DCE9F2  | #1B3A5C       | #E84E1B, #FFFFFF            |

Rules for all palettes: the mascot's body stays light (per its value rules);
eyes are the darkest note on the body; the accent never spreads to more
than ~20% of the frame.
