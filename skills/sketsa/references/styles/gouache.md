# Style: gouache

Opaque painterly poster paint: thick matte color patches, visible brush
edges, flat but hand-made.

- Rendering: matte flat fills with slightly wobbly brush boundaries; 2–4
  tones per shape (a base, a shadow patch, a highlight patch). No gradients,
  no glazing, no photoreal textures. White is paint, not paper showing.
- Edges stay soft-cornered and organic; nothing ruler-straight.
- Composition: poster hierarchy — big shapes behind, mascot sharp in front,
  one accent element in a complementary color.

Composition sentence: layered flats with the mascot one tone brighter than
