# Style: woodcut

Carved-block print: bold shapes cut from high-contrast ink, visible carving
marks, dramatic black/white balance.

- Rendering: 2–3 tones only (paper, mid-tone hatching, full ink). All edges
  are carving edges — angular, confident, slightly rough. Texture comes
  from hatching and gouge marks, never from gradients or soft shading.
- The mascot reads through silhouette first: its light body against a heavy
  ink field (or the reverse in the dark palette).
- Composition: strong diagonal or central medallion layouts; negative space
  is carved as deliberately as the subject.
