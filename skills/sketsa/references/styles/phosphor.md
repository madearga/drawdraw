# Style: phosphor

Glowing CRT terminal: single-color phosphor linework on a near-black glass
screen, scanlines, slight bloom.

- Rendering: all linework is the phosphor color with a soft 1–2px bloom
  (this is the one style allowed to glow). Background is near-black with
  faint scanlines and vignette. Secondary elements drop to dimmer opacity
  of the same phosphor color; the accent color is used only once per image
  (a warning state, a highlighted element).
- Text-like glyphs are allowed as abstract decoration (blurred blocks), but
  never readable words unless the user asked for them.
- Composition: the mascot is the brightest object; everything recedes by
  brightness, not by hue.

Composition sentence: a dark room, one glowing shape — the mascot — doing
the work.
