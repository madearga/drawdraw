# Layout playbook

Pick the layout per shot from the user's request. All layouts keep the
mascot as the actor — the image is about what the mascot DOES.

## editorial (default)
Single scene, one metaphor for the idea. Mascot mid-action, centered with
room to breathe. 1:1 for social, 16:9 for blog headers. No text in the
image unless the user asked for it.

## flowchart
The idea as 3–5 steps, drawn as connected stations; the mascot moves
through them left-to-right. Same visual weight per station; arrows are
simple structure-ink lines with the accent used only on the "current" step
concept.

## mini-comic
Three panels, read left-to-right: setup → complication → payoff. Panel
borders are structure-ink rectangles on paper. The mascot's eyes and pose
carry the emotion; no speech bubbles unless asked, and then two words max.

## explainer
How-it-works cutaway: the mascot walks through 2–4 labeled stages of a
mechanism (arrows, boxes, or layers — drawn in the style's own language).
The mascot is the guide, present in every stage; annotations are minimal
geometric marks, not paragraphs of text.

## Expressing motion
Motion is pose and context (lean, hop, press, tilt), never speed lines or
motion blur (unless the pack's style allows them). Props are minimal —
each extra prop dilutes the metaphor.
